Rules (from PRODUCT.md):
- Photos presented as the academy, its classes, students or events must be the academy's own.
- Identifiable students need their own or a parent's consent.
- Badges and marks (Suro Bharati, ISO) need the real artwork from the issuing body. The ISO logo itself may not be used by certified organisations.
